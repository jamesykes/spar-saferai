5. Updated Results Excerpt From report/main.tex

Table 1 summarises the locked policy comparison. All six policies were run in the same locked_v8_hidden_reveal experiment, using 10 reveal seeds and the budget grid [45,90,180,360,720,1200,1798]. The report JSON records mode=LOCKED_V8_HIDDEN_REVEAL, uses_hidden_reveal_orders=true, and settings_reduced_from_requested=false.

Under the corrected hidden reveal-order protocol, exploration-bonus c=1.0 achieved the lowest average AUC, 0.009409, over the selected budget grid. The next lowest average AUC values were exploration-bonus c=0.5 at 0.010175, epsilon-greedy at 0.010320, greedy LOO-fragility at 0.011005, and the uniform baseline at 0.011075. Exploration-bonus c=0.25 performed worse, with average AUC 0.017137. However, all fragility-guided policies had paired win fractions below 0.5 against the uniform baseline: 0.386 for epsilon-greedy, 0.386 for exploration-bonus c=1.0, 0.371 for greedy LOO-fragility, 0.357 for exploration-bonus c=0.5, and 0.286 for exploration-bonus c=0.25. The AUC advantage is therefore aggregation-dependent and should be interpreted cautiously.

At budget 1200, the mean L1 imbalances are 0.0 for uniform, 459.8 for epsilon-greedy, 516.0 for exploration-bonus c=1.0, 569.6 for exploration-bonus c=0.5, 595.4 for greedy LOO-fragility, and 627.2 for exploration-bonus c=0.25. Thus concentration remains severe at intermediate budgets for the fragility-guided policies. At budget 1798, all policies have mean, median, and maximum L1 imbalance equal to zero, with minimum step count 199 and maximum step count 200. This convergence is expected because the full available fitted SOTA dataset has been revealed.


6. Updated Discussion and Limitations Excerpt From report/main.tex

Under the corrected hidden reveal-order protocol, exploration-bonus c=1.0 achieved the lowest average AUC over the selected budget grid. However, all fragility-guided policies had paired win fractions below 0.5 against the uniform baseline. This suggests that the fragility signal is useful in some runs and budgets, but not uniformly reliable.

The locked run therefore provides weak-to-moderate evidence that fragility-guided policies can improve aggregate recovery error, especially when paired with a stronger exploration bonus. The evidence remains mixed across paired comparisons. The policies with better AUC also concentrate elicitation effort strongly at intermediate budgets. LOO output fragility can identify influential or unstable inputs, but using it too directly risks over-concentration. Exploration-aware variants partially mitigate this problem, with the c=1.0 exploration-bonus setting looking best among the tested values in the locked run.

Key limitations now listed include:
- the locked policy run used approximate LOO-term subsampling with max_loo_terms_per_step=20;
- the locked run used 10 reveal seeds;
- no fragility-guided policy beat uniform on a majority of paired seed-budget comparisons;
- concentration remains severe at intermediate budgets for the fragility-guided policies;
- all policies necessarily converge at full budget 1798 because the full available fitted SOTA dataset has been revealed;
- runtime constraints shaped the experiment design, including batching of fragility recomputation.
